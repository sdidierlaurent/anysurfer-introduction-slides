Introduction to digital accessibility

Accessibility

Accessible:
Perceivable, understandable, and operable by everyone,
including people with disabilities.

Everyone can use your website or mobile app, including people who:

  • Have a disability
  • Are aging
  • Have circumstantial limitations

How do we reach accessibility?

Where to start:

  • Raise awareness and follow trainings
  • Add accessibility as a requirement for every new website, document, or application
  • Choose suppliers (or be a supplier) with proven expertise in digital accessibility
  • When using existing widgets, choose accessible ones
  • After delivery, check that all accessibility requirements are fulfilled

Follow standards:

  • Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.2)
  • W3C-WAI ARIA Authoring Practices Guide
  • For websites, mobile apps, digital documents, newsletters, social media,...

Legislation and standards

2 european directives

  • Web Accessibility directive : websites and apps of public sector
  • European Accessibility Act : certain common products and services

Web Accessibility Directive

  • European directive: Full text in Official Journal (available in all EU languages)
  • Websites and mobile applications maintained by public sector bodies must be(come) accessible.
  • They must publish an accessibility statement

European Accessibility Act

  • Businesses will need to comply with the EAA by June 2025.
  • Applies to products and services:
    • Products: computers, smartphones, ATMs, payment terminals, e-readers,...
    • Services : 112 number, telecom, banks, public transport, e-commerce,...
  • Several transpositions into belgian law (per theme)

Accessibility statement
